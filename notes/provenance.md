# Sequence provenance: what each dataset can and cannot identify (#61)

**Date:** 2026-10-06
**Status:** audited — the provenance specification, sibling of the measurement
contract (`notes/estimands.md`: that note says *what* is estimated, this one
says *where each row came from*)
**Evidence:** #21–#26 plus this audit — `scripts/triage/sequence_inventory.py`
→ `data/triage/sequence_inventory.csv` (the matrix) and
`notes/sequence_inventory.json` (per-file evidence)
**Answers:** PLAN.md open questions 1 and 3
**Blocks:** Phase 2 step 3 (provenance columns), Phase 4B (sequence model),
and any longitudinal or intervention claim anywhere in the plan

**Verdict.** MyceliumSeg has **no usable longitudinal sequence**: no ordered
frames, no dish identity, no capture interval — the three facts a sequence
claim needs are all absent, and this audit adds nothing that rescues them
(§ 4.1). The cross-sectional fallback of `notes/estimands.md` § 3 therefore
stands unchanged for the anchor release, and dynamic and intervention models
move to the nominated datasets: **Fricker/Boddy** for growth over real time and
grazer-treatment contrasts (causal interpretation depends on assignment details), ***Podospora*** movies for per-frame growth form
and the manipulated medium contrast, and a **controlled rig** for a
species × medium causal design, with the *Physarum* scanner set as the
publishing template (§ 5).

Every number below is regenerated by the audit; the committed CSV carries the
full evidence strings per row. `validate_rows()` refuses any row whose
estimand claims contradict its own provenance — no interval → no rate, no
order → no per-step change, no specimen identity → no intervention — and
`tests/test_sequence_inventory.py` re-runs that guard against the committed
matrix, so the note, the CSV and the code cannot drift apart.

## 1. The six provenance facts

| Fact | The question | Without it, you cannot identify |
|---|---|---|
| **Ordered frames** | Are frames in a defensible order *independent of the file numbering*? | `delta_per_ordered_step` (estimands § 4) |
| **Specimen / dish identity** | Can a frame be attributed to one physical specimen with a replicate label? | within-specimen change, per-dish effects, any intervention contrast |
| **Capture interval** | Real elapsed time between frames — not playback fps, not zip mtime, not export order | `physical_rate` (mm/day, log/day); a frame index is not a duration |
| **Resolution** | Pixel geometry per image or per block; one geometry or several | calibrated mm², boundary estimands, measurement-error budgets |
| **Duplicates** | Byte-identical frames shipped under one or two ids | honest uncertainty and honest differences — de-duplicate *before* splitting or differencing (estimands § 7) |
| **Condition labels** | Species / medium / treatment / day / temperature per image, and whether they are right | strata and contrasts — a wrong label poisons exactly the comparison it labels |

`physical_rate` on `fricker_grazers` means per **observed elapsed day** (EXIF
capture times), while the filename day label is retained separately. The two
clocks agree on order, but the first filename gap and the papers' descriptions
of the baseline schedule do not agree exactly; see § 4.2 and
`notes/fricker_grazers_analysis.md`. `delta_per_ordered_step` on a movie means
per **frame**. For the six 2023 *Podospora* films, Figshare supplies the
18-minute capture interval and 1.63 µm/source-pixel scale, so within-movie rates
can be expressed per hour and in physical image units relative to the first
frame. That does not establish elapsed time since germination or causal
identification of the M0/M2 contrast. The interval, scale, and CC BY 4.0 licence
are documented in `data/raw/podospora_thallus/SOURCE.md`.

## 4. What the audit found, dataset by dataset

### 4.1 `myceliumseg` — no sequence; Gate C stands

The audit re-derives the Gate C inputs from the stored triage outputs rather
than rescanning 56 GB, and every number matches the #21–#26 record: 20,176
images, **0** with `DateTimeOriginal`, forward-step fractions 0.489–0.547
(id→mtime) and 0.483–0.552 (colony size vs order — an independent signal),
133 byte-identical images in 62 groups, 18,487 known labels against 1,689
blank, and **20 md5 groups whose two copies carry different non-blank labels**
— exactly the `PO`≡`MYG` and `PDA`≡`labeled-GL` collisions of #23, where the
pixels say one thing and the folder says another.

Nothing here was improved or lost: MyceliumSeg remains a cross-sectional
release. Frame-indexed change stays *defined but not estimated*
(`notes/estimands.md` § 4), and the five Gate C preconditions remain unmet.

### 4.2 `fricker_grazers` — the longitudinal and intervention anchor

* **777 kept images** (plus 3 rejected 600×450 thumbnails in `Pv/rejected/`)
  parse into **155 series** = species × treatment × replicate; every filename
  carries its axes.
* **Order is demonstrated, not assumed:** all 777 kept images have EXIF
  `DateTimeOriginal` (Nikon COOLPIX P80, capture dates 2009-02-24 →
  2009-10-25), and as the filename day increases the EXIF time never goes
  backwards — **155/155 series confirmed, 0 conflicts**.
* **Interval:** all 777 JPEGs have EXIF capture time and form 622 adjacent
  within-series transitions. One additional processed workbook has no linked
  JPEG. For the transitions with increasing filename day labels, observed gap
  medians are 0.923 days (label gap 1), 1.965 (gap 2), 4.028 (gap 4), 5.946
  (gap 6), 7.889 (gap 8), and 14.208 (gap 14). There are no capture-time
  reversals. The first gap differs from the original paper's stated 2-day
  post-addition schedule and needs to remain explicit rather than silently
  mapped to days since treatment.
* **New finding — 4 byte-identical pairs:** `Pi_ctrl2_d1_{1..4}.jpg` ≡
  `Pi_ctrl2_d2_{1..4}.jpg` (same md5 *and* same EXIF second). The fifth `ctrl2`
  replicate's two files differ in bytes but share the same EXIF second, so all
  five `ctrl2` series capture "day 1" and "day 2" in one session. Day 2 of Pi
  `ctrl2` is therefore **not an independent observation**: differencing it
  against day 1 reports Δ = 0 growth that was never measured. The duplicates
  are named in `notes/sequence_inventory.json`
  (`duplicate_groups_detail`) and must be dropped under the estimands § 7
  sequence rule before any change is computed.
* One processed workbook (`Pv_ctrl1_d4_4`) has no original JPEG/EXIF match.
  Resolutions are two-tier: 3648×2736 (572) and 2048×1536 (205, all Pi) —
  keep the scale node per image when measuring.
* The 2011 methods report that trays were **randomly repositioned weekly**.
  They do not report random allocation of trays to grazer treatments. The
  available files support repeated-measures models and species-stratified
  treatment-group contrasts; causal `intervention_effect` claims remain
  conditional on assignment/exchangeability evidence not present in the
  described methods.
* The original paper reports photographs before grazer addition and then at
  2, 4, 8, 16, 24, 38, 52, 66, and 80 days. The later reanalysis reports
  baseline plus 2, 4, 8, 16, and 32 days. This Zenodo subset has filename day
  labels 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 24, and 38, with EXIF gaps generally matching label
  differences, except the label-1 to label-2 interval. Preserve filename label
  and EXIF elapsed time as separate fields.

### 4.3 `fricker_networks` — cross-species snapshots

The staged Zenodo release has 33 JPEGs and 33 matching parameter files across
11 coded taxa, three samples per code; each colony appears once, so there is no
within-colony sequence. The paper describes 12 taxa and three replicates per
strain. However, the pinned author-published analysis RDS has 36 real-network
rows across 12 taxa with counts 1, 3, or 4 per taxon, four IDs absent from the
Zenodo parameter archive, and one archive ID without an exact table match.
Labels occur across colonies rather than within a colony. Microscopic
Ascomycota/Zygomycetous hyphae and macroscopic Basidiomycota cords were imaged
under different substrate, temperature, time, and pixel-scale regimes. The
dataset supports cautious study-specific cross-sectional summaries of its
author-defined network traits, but not growth rates or a generic prior for
MyceliumSeg's segmentation and area outcomes. See
`notes/fricker_network_snapshots.md` and the reproducible audit script.

### 4.4 `podospora_thallus` — ordered, identified, calibrated; interval external to AVI

Six MJPEG movies (85/85/81/74/70/69 = **464 frames**), and the container is
internally complete everywhere: header frame count == stored `movi` chunks in
**6/6** movies, 0 duplicate frame bytes, ~7,125×6,986–7,220×6,973 per movie.
Filenames give medium (M0 vs M2) and replicate, with n = 3 movies per medium.
The Figshare description for this exact six-movie release specifies 18 min
between images, 1.63 µm per source pixel, bright-field acquisition at 27 °C,
and nine-tile mosaics. The Figshare record is CC BY 4.0. These values are
external to the AVI; its 5 fps is playback only. At the stated cadence, the
movies span 20.4–25.2 h between the first and last frame. The time of the first
frame relative to germination is not specified, so rates can be reported per
hour relative to that frame, but not as lag since germination. The 2023 paper
independently describes roughly 18-minute sampling and about 1 µm spatial
resolution. The M0/M2 comparison is descriptive because the reviewed methods
do not document randomized condition assignment. See
`data/raw/podospora_thallus/SOURCE.md` and `notes/podospora_growth_model.md`.

### 4.5 `podospora_m0_raw` — 85 advertised pages, 15 frames with content

The ImageJ header says `images=85`, and the arithmetic agrees: the file size
equals `3,789 + 85 × 50,131,345 + 10,584` bytes exactly (single-strip layout,
stale trailer — the IFD chain itself is broken and points into zeros, which is
why `tifffile` sees only 2 pages). **But only pages 1–15 carry image data:
pages 16–85 are zero-filled** — 70 blank pages, one shared hash, content
hashes all distinct. This corrects the "85-frame stack" reading of the release
(`data/INVENTORY.md`): treat the movie as **15 frames**, or a Δ over the blank
region silently reports 0. The order check on the 15 content frames behaves as
a real sequence should (adjacent mean |Δ| 9.50 vs 18.55 for distant pairs).

### 4.6 `podospora_net2019a` / `podospora_net2019b` — small but clean

The 82-frame downsized AVI is complete (82 == 82) with 0 duplicate frames at
1,604×1,708; its 4 fps is playback timing. The 11-page RGB TIFF
(2,524×1,986×3, `images=11`) has 0 duplicates, and adjacent pages differ less
than distant ones (12.6 vs 17.6 mean |Δ|) — consistent with frames 50–60 of
one run, though the filename is the only evidence for that reading. One
colony each; no interval in either file.

### 4.7 `physarum_rig` — what full provenance looks like

One of the 24 release archives is on disk (`physarum-001-dish0`, of 51,036
frames total): 1,696 frames with **strictly increasing UTC-nanosecond
timestamps**, gaps of 300–301 s against a nominal 300 s, a per-frame source
PNG sha256 (0 duplicates), qc flags (0 flagged), a fitted dish circle on all
1,696 frames, and a mm/px scale from the 90-mm dish fit (0.089286). Dish
identity is in the filename. This is exactly the per-frame provenance bundle
— timestamp, content hash, QC, registration — to adopt for our own rig, and
the reason the release is nominated as the publishing template. It is not
fungal and carries no randomized intervention, so it supplies dynamics, not
treatment effects.

### 4.8 `aureobasidium` — no data

Zenodo 14001310 contains a conference book of abstracts only; nothing to
inventory (`data_files: []` in the JSON).

## 6. Provenance rules this audit adds

1. **De-duplicate before differencing — in every dataset, not only
   MyceliumSeg.** The four `Pi_ctrl2` pairs are the worked example: naive
   differencing turns a copy into "zero growth".
2. **Never promote a machine artefact to a clock.** File numbering (#22),
   zip mtimes (#21), AVI playback fps, and the ImageJ page count alone are
   not capture intervals. The interval column must name its source:
   *filename day + EXIF*, *timestamp array*, *external (paper)*, or *none*.
3. **Frame counts need a content check, not just a header.** Header == stored
   chunks (AVI), arithmetic == ImageJ header *and* content hashes (TIFF),
   timestamps == source sha256 (OME-Zarr). `podospora_m0_raw` fails the naive
   version of this check and only the hash pass exposed it.
4. **Label conflicts are counted, not argued.** `label_conflicts()` reports
   md5 groups where two copies carry different non-blank labels; MyceliumSeg's
   value is 20, matching #23 exactly.
5. **Optional DEFLATE64 support.** `Zygomycetous1.zip` stores compression
   method 9; with `zipfile-deflate64` installed the audit hashes all six
   entries, without it they land in `unreadable_entries` instead of silently
   disappearing.

## Reproducing

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/triage/sequence_inventory.py           # full audit (~30 s warm)
.venv/bin/python scripts/triage/sequence_inventory.py --no-hashes --limit 5   # smoke test
.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_sequence_inventory.py -q   # parser/container/guard tests
.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests -q                             # full suite
ruff check scripts/triage/sequence_inventory.py
```

Outputs: `data/triage/sequence_inventory.csv` (9 rows, the matrices of § 2–3)
and `notes/sequence_inventory.json` (per-series, per-file evidence behind
every cell). `--no-hashes` skips content hashing and marks the duplicates
column *not checked*; the committed artifacts were produced with hashing on.

Surrounding record: `notes/gate.md` (Gate C), `notes/estimands.md`
(measurement contract), `data/INVENTORY.md` (dataset-by-dataset acquisition
